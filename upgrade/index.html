A few quick answers
What's included?
Every course on Art GPS — Skills and Creation — including new courses added during your membership year. Art History is always free for everyone.
How does the yearly plan work?
Your membership renews once a year. You can cancel anytime from the link in your Stripe receipt email, and you keep access until the end of the year you paid for.
Will my saved work and progress carry over?
Yes. Journal entries, badges, and saved cards stay with your account. The free lessons you've already opened remain accessible too.
Can I switch options later?
Yes. Send us a note at hello@feiteamart.com and we'll help adjust your access.
How does the online small group class work?
Groups meet live on Zoom with the teacher, with a maximum of 4 students per group. Each live course is priced separately. After you enroll, we'll be in touch about the next group's schedule.
