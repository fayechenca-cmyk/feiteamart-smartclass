Welcome to Color Practice! Let's play with paint and see what we can discover.
Start with red, yellow, blue, black, and white. A little paint can take you a long way!
Watch how real paint behaves. Try a little more water, or a little more color.
Mix, paint, and compare. Change the amounts, and watch a whole new color appear!
Save your discoveries in your own palette. You're learning to notice tiny differences.
Next, explore Color Neighbors. Use lighter and darker colors to make simple shapes feel round and solid.
Then try Complementary Colors. Discover how opposite colors help you paint shadows and reflected light.
No need to get it perfect. Try something, be curious, and have fun! Let's start mixing.
