Art History is always free.

FEI TeamArt provides a learning sequence, guides, activities, and progress tools around independently published resources. All videos and readings can be browsed without payment or completion of a Challenge. Challenges record progress through the guided path.

Videos and publisher rights

Videos use the publishers’ YouTube players, with credit and a link to the original page. We do not download or rehost videos. The YouTube Terms permit playback through service features; YouTube’s embedding guidance explains the player. This is not an open license to copy videos or the artworks within them. Publisher settings and regional restrictions can change.

On 15 October 2026, all 34 Asian-path video URLs returned a player through YouTube’s official oEmbed endpoint. This verifies the endpoint and publisher metadata, not uninterrupted playback in every location or a legal clearance of every depicted artwork. Use the original publisher link if a player is unavailable. Most videos are in English; Yang Yongliang’s interview uses Chinese speech with English subtitles. Some resources are extended lectures; watch in sections.

Historical images

Gallery images have individual public-domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA records. Each lesson links to the image source and applicable license, with creator credit. Local copies are reduced-size previews with original proportions preserved. An ancient artwork and a modern photograph have separate rights; the listed license refers to the particular image used.

Modern art teaching diagrams

Seven simple vector diagrams were drawn specifically for this course to illustrate studio practice, printing, ink, text, urban growth, networks, and electronic screens. They contain no artist portraits or reproductions of specific protected artworks. They are labeled as teaching diagrams; original works remain available through museum, artist, and publisher links. The Western path’s separate AI illustration notes explain its concept images.
