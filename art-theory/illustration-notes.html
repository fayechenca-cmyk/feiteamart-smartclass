Illustrations for exploring ideas

Lessons 29–36 use AI-assisted concept illustrations created for FEI TeamArt on October 14, 2026. These are teaching visuals, not historical artworks, reproductions, artist portraits, or evidence of an artist’s endorsement.

The images were generated from written descriptions of course concepts, without supplying photographs or reference artworks. The prompts requested new compositions and excluded identifiable existing artworks, real people, logos and text. They are not labelled public domain or Creative Commons. AI generation does not establish copyright clearance or exclusive copyright ownership.

For actual artworks and their historical context, follow the museum links in each lesson’s Gallery & Sources.

29 · Abstract Expressionism

Gesture, movement and expressive colour, shown through independently composed ribbons and arcs.

30 · Pop Art

Unbranded everyday objects and bold colour blocks introduce mass culture and visual repetition.

31 · Minimalism

Simple geometric volumes and open space draw attention to form and proportion.

32 · Conceptual Art

A frame, a thought bubble and a blank card suggest the relationship between an idea and an object.

33 · Performance & Body Art

A fictional, faceless figure and an arc of movement introduce action as a medium.

34 · Postmodernism

Newly composed paper shapes and contrasting fragments explore layering and recombination.

35 · Global Contemporary Art

Threads and abstract doorways suggest identity, exchange and connection.

36 · New Media & AI

Pixels, branching grids and geometric tiles introduce the relationship between computation and images.
