# Preparation guided preview

Preserves the original Preparation sequence in 23 small stages: welcome, materials (including real paper), workspace, optional taping, posture, grips, teacher hatching demonstration and five practice prompts, perspective, hand warm-up, and continuation to Cube.

Four brief encouragement moments acknowledge learner-confirmed preparation and practice, not artistic proficiency. No time limits. Artchi replays the voice; sound can be muted. Teacher videos stream from the original four Cloudflare IDs; durations come from their HLS playlists. Images are the existing lesson references, toolkit and credited paper photography. English synthetic narration uses Ava, matching the Cube prototype; it does not impersonate the teacher.

Independent design preview: local device progress only, no official badge or account-progress writes. Published route /lessons/preparation/guided/. Original lesson remains available through the back arrow.
